Scope, planning, and key facts

A useful first step is to make an item list and describe the pickup and destination locations. Note anything that could affect carrying, loading, or placement, then ask how the move would be organized. A provider can use those details to discuss the service and identify questions that need answers before moving day.

Key facts to clarify:

  • What it covers: Confirm which business items and tasks the mover will handle, including any equipment that needs special attention.
  • Typical timing: The schedule depends on the move’s size, distance, access, and agreed plan. Confirm timing directly rather than relying on a general estimate.
  • Cost factors: The amount and type of property, travel distance, access conditions, and requested services can affect the overall cost. Ask what information the provider needs to discuss it; no price is assumed here.
  • Provider fit: Compare providers by asking the same questions about scope, handling, scheduling, and what is not included. Get the answers in writing when you arrange the job.
