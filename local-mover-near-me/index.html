How to prepare and what the terms mean

Have the basic details ready: the type of move, the starting point, the destination, and whether you want help with a piano or storage. The amount of belongings and the distance between locations can affect the plan. A provider may ask you to describe what is moving so they can understand the scope. Confirm the details directly, especially if you have needs beyond standard household or business items.

Here are a few terms you may hear:
- Local move: moving belongings within the provider’s local service area.
- Long-distance move: transportation beyond the local area. Ask the company how it categorizes your destination.
- Storage facility: a place where belongings can be kept instead of delivered straight to the final address.
- Access: the practical arrangements for getting items in or out at the locations. Explain any details that could affect moving items.

There isn't one typical schedule that fits every job. The quantity of belongings, travel, and services all affect how a move is planned. Share those details when asking providers about timing and cost.
